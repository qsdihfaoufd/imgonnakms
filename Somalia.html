How did I identify MEDIA BIAS

LANGUAGE USE

  • Uses hopeful and optimistic language about Somalia’s future.
  • May encourage viewers to believe that positive change is possible.
  • Could also make some viewers think the situation is better than it actually is.
EMOTIONAL APPEAL
  • Appeals to hope and perseverance.
  • Creates a contrast with earlier scenes of chaos and conflict.
  • May confuse viewers because the peaceful beach scene feels very different from the hardships shown before.
VISUALS
  • Shows a calm beach with families and people enjoying themselves.
  • Gives viewers a more positive image of Somalia.
  • Could make the audience believe that conditions are improving.
CREDIBILITY OF SOURCES
  • Shows footage from 2012 and 2021.
  • Allows viewers to compare Somalia's situation over time.
  • Helps show some signs of improvement.

Reflections

  • Different words, visuals, or sources could create a completely different impression.
  • Showing only destruction and violence could make Somalia seem hopeless.
  • Showing communities, beaches, and people supporting each other presents a more hopeful perspective.
