Visuals and Footages

why is it important in the media?

✦ Visuals and footages alludes to images, video recordings and/or clips from media that provides a visual presentation to a certain event or situation.

They are important because...

  • provide evidence
  • create an impact (e.g. emotional response, public opinions)
  • Attract attention
  • help with visualization and understanding
  • May be misleading at times (e.g. taken out of context, framing or edited)

✦

Key takeaways

Images Influence how audiences interpret events

Negative potrayal

  • selective curation and cropping: cropping or choosing certain images can change how the audience views the situation
  • Emotional amplification: Dramatic or graphic images can trigger emotions before the audience understands the situation.
  • Stereotyping and Generalization Images can reinforce negative stereotypes about certain groups or locations.

Positive potrayal

  • Selective Facial Expressions: Showing positive or negative expressions can make people appear more favorable or unfavorable.
  • Controlled Framing and Composition Camera angles, lighting, and image size can influence how trustworthy or powerful someone appears.
  • Reinforcing Identity and pre-existing beliefs Images can strengthen the audience's existing beliefs and create positive or negative perceptions.
